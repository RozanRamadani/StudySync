# StudySync Product Requirements Document

## Product Overview

StudySync is an AI-powered study duration recommendation platform that helps students determine the optimal study duration based on:

* Focus Level
* Fatigue Level
* Material Complexity

The recommendation engine uses Fuzzy Logic Mamdani with 27 fuzzy rules.

---

## Target Users

Primary Users:

* Junior High School Students
* Senior High School Students
* University Students

Secondary Users:

* Tutors
* Mentors
* Independent Learners

---

## Core Problem

Students often struggle to determine the ideal study duration because their cognitive condition changes throughout the day.

Traditional methods such as Pomodoro are static and do not consider:

* Focus level
* Fatigue level
* Material difficulty

---
